About Our Technical Summit

Driving Engineering Excellence & Innovation

ABESIT Ghaziabad provides a platform for students, faculty, and industry professionals to collaborate, build impactful technical solutions, and accelerate startup ideas.

Hackathons & Coding

Engage in 36-hour intense hackathons. Tackle real-world problems spanning AI, Smart Mobility, Cyber Security, and FinTech with round-the-clock mentorship.

Hands-on Workshops

Practical lab-oriented masterclasses on Generative AI, Cloud Native DevOps, Machine Learning, and Robotics led by veteran engineers and researchers.

FDPs for Educators

One-week Faculty Development Programs updating college professors on emerging industry workflows, research methodologies, and cloud architectures.

Day 1 - 12:00 PM

Hacking Begins: Round 1 Sprint

36-hour non-stop hacking commences. Mentors from Google Cloud and Microsoft review project architectures.

Day 2 - 09:00 AM

Prototype Submission & Semi-Final Demos

GitHub repository freeze, code quality verification, and jury demo evaluations.

Day 2 - 04:00 PM

Grand Finale Pitch & Prize Distribution

Top 10 finalist stage presentations, announcement of winners, and ₹1,50,000 prize distribution.
